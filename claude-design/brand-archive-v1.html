Why these two

  • Free for commercial use. Both shipped with the SIL Open Font License.
  • Full Latin Extended. Kiswahili diacritics (Ñ, Á, Ä, Ö, etc.) and the long-vowel marks that show up in some place-names.
  • Tabular figures everywhere. A 1 and an 8 take the same horizontal space — vote tallies line up vertically without a designer doing anything.
  • Low-DPI Android safe. Public Sans was designed for US government web; tested on low-resolution rendering. Plex Mono has visible terminals at small sizes.
  • Mono is reserved. Codes, tallies, timestamps, hashes. Never body copy.

System · disclaimer
The disclaimer system

Calm. Unmissable. Uncroppable.

Every results view shows a persistent strip directly under the OS status bar (so it's in any screenshot) plus a monogram watermark on shareable charts. Designed to never look alarmist — but also never absent.

Persistent strip — three tones

● Citizen tally · Not IEBC
● Provisional · 68.8% of stations reporting
● Offline · last refresh 14 min ago

Watermark on charts

National Vote Share — President · 68.8% reporting
kurazetu · citizen tally · not iebc

Rules

  • Always above the fold. The strip is part of the status-bar-adjacent chrome, not a banner that can scroll away.
  • No emoji, no exclamation marks. Mono caps, single dot. Reads as a state indicator, not a warning.
  • Tone is contextual. Default neutral; ochre when results are provisional; brick when fully offline / stale > 30 min.
  • Charts carry a watermark. Bottom-right, 60% opacity. Stays even if the rest of the screenshot is cropped.
  • First-launch full-screen intercept. Detailed disclaimer + What it IS / IS NOT table (see onboarding flow).
