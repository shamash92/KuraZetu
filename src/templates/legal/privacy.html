What we collect

  • Your phone number.
  • Profile details you choose to give: name, age, gender, and polling centre.
  • Photographs of results forms you upload.
  • The location of a pin you place or a photograph you take, collected only while the app is open.
  • An identifier for your phone and an address for push notifications.
  • Website visits, through Google Analytics.

Why we collect it

To run your account, check uploads, and prevent abuse.

What is public

Uploaded forms and polling centre locations are public, without who sent them. Your phone number is never shown to anyone.

Who processes it for us

  • Africa's Talking sends verification codes by SMS.
  • Expo and Google Firebase deliver push notifications.
  • Amazon Web Services stores photographs.
  • Google Maps shows maps in the app.
  • Contabo hosts our servers.

Deleting your account

Delete your account and its personal data on the account deletion page. Uploaded forms stay published without your name or phone number.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to see, correct, or delete your personal data. Write to shamash@tutamail.com.
